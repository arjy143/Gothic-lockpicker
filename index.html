Gothic Lockpick Solver

Slide every gate until its hole sits on the keyway. Nothing may ever slide past the last hole.

The lock

6

Set the lock up the way the game shows it: tap a hole, nudge with ◀ ▶, or pick a gate and slide it with the ← → arrow keys (↑ ↓ chooses the gate).

Linked gates: the numbers under each gate are the other gates it drags along. Tap to cycle: off → same (moves the same way) → opp (moves the opposite way).
